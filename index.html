A Poem for Baby 💙

🌷
Sometimes I stare at my phone,
Waiting for your name to light up my screen,
Like it's the only proof
That distance isn't winning against us.
You're far from me now,
But somehow you still feel close
In every late-night thought
And in every song that suddenly reminds me of you.
And I keep wondering
If I could reach through the miles
Would you still feel the same way
The way I do when I miss you?
Because even oceans between us
Can't change what you are to me
The one who turned waiting
Into something worth it.
You're my angel on the other side of the world
And I'm just here
Holding on to every message
Every call, every "I miss you"
Like it's enough to keep me going.
And maybe that's what love is
Not being close all the time
But choosing each other
Even when we're miles apart.
